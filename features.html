Pregnancy app features

Complete Prenatal Care & Tracking Tools

MiGynae combines hospital-grade tracking with medically reviewed education — every tool you need from positive test to postpartum.

Medical-Grade Q&A

Search pregnancy symptoms and get evidence-based answers reviewed by certified healthcare professionals. Updated with current OBGYN guidelines — from morning sickness to third-trimester concerns.

Advanced Kick Counter

Log fetal movements daily, view history over weeks, and spot pattern changes. Share records at antenatal visits. Intelligent reminders help you stay consistent from week 28 onward.

Precision Contraction Timer

One-tap start/stop timing with duration and interval analysis. Pattern recognition helps you know when contractions are regular enough to call your doctor or head to the hospital.

Personalized Nutrition Plans

Trimester-specific meal plans with Indian diet charts, traditional superfoods (ghee, dates, millets), sample daily menus, and clear do's and don'ts for vegetarian and non-vegetarian mothers.

Safe Exercise Programs

Certified prenatal workouts by trimester — walking, pelvic tilts, squats, breathing exercises, and cultural tips for Indian moms. Safety protocols tell you when to stop.

Weekly Pregnancy Updates

See your baby's size each week, track body changes, and get actionable tips matched to your gestational age. Personalized from your due date at setup.

Support Across All Three Trimesters

The app adapts content and tools to your current pregnancy week.

First Trimester

Weeks 1–12

  • Nausea-friendly diet & safe food lists
  • Daily activity guidance (Pregnancy 101)
  • Early symptom Q&A

Second Trimester

Weeks 13–27

  • Weekly baby development updates
  • Nutrition & exercise by trimester
  • Kick counting begins when recommended

Third Trimester

Weeks 28–40+

  • Full kick counter & contraction timer
  • 9th-month diet & labour prep exercises
  • Links to free labour & postpartum guides
