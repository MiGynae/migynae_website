Nutrition

An Indian pregnancy diet plan, trimester by trimester

Our Indian pregnancy diet plan app features meals designed around what's actually available in Indian homes — dal, roti, sabzi, curd, and seasonal fruits — with nutrient breakdowns approved by gynecologists.

First Trimester

Combat nausea, build foundations

Small, frequent meals to manage morning sickness. Focus on folate, iron, and B6 — critical for neural tube development in weeks 1–13.

Methi paratha Masoor dal Coconut water Banana Curd rice
Second Trimester

Build bone, brain & muscle

Calcium, DHA, and protein take centre stage as your baby's skeleton, brain, and muscles grow rapidly through weeks 14–27.

Paneer Ragi porridge Palak sabzi Moong sprouts Til chikki
Third Trimester

Prepare for labour & breastfeeding

High-fibre, iron-rich foods to prevent constipation and top up iron stores. Calories increase slightly to fuel your baby's final growth surge.

Khichdi Dates Lauki soup Rajma Dry fruits

Doctor Community

Real answers from real gynecologists

Every question in the MiGynae community is reviewed by a verified gynecologist. Ask anything — from diet concerns to labour signs — and get an evidence-based answer, not just crowd opinions.

Thousands of questions are already answered and searchable. If yours is already there, you get an instant AI-assisted response drawing from our doctor-verified archive.

"Is it safe to eat papaya during the first trimester?"
Doctor Answered
Raw papaya contains latex which can trigger uterine contractions and should be avoided. Ripe papaya in small amounts is generally considered safe after the first trimester.
"How many kicks should I feel in third trimester?"
Doctor Answered
Most guidelines recommend at least 10 movements within 2 hours. Use the baby movement tracker in the app and contact your doctor if you notice a significant decrease.
"My periods are irregular — how do I track ovulation?"
Doctor Answered
With irregular cycles, tracking is harder by calendar alone. Log your symptoms daily in the app — the AI learns your personal patterns and improves predictions over time.
