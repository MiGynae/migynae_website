During Labour

What to know when labour begins

When the contractions are here, keep it simple. A few things make the biggest difference in the moment - save these.

The tips that matter most
1
Breathe slow, don't panic
Take a slow, deep breath in and let it out slowly. Panic-breathing makes pain feel worse - steady breathing calms your body and lowers the pain. This is your single biggest tool.
2
Keep moving, change position
Walk, sway, or rock on a birthing ball. Upright positions - squatting, leaning forward, lying on your side - help your baby move down.
3
Do NOT push early
Even if you feel the urge, push only when your doctor tells you to. Pushing too soon tires you out and can cause tearing.
4
Rest and sip between contractions
Use the gaps to relax your shoulders and jaw, breathe, and sip water. Saving energy now helps you push better later.
5
It's okay to ask for pain relief
If pain feels unbearable or labour is long, you can ask about an epidural. Used correctly, it is a safe option - there is no prize for suffering.
