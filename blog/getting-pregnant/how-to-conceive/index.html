How to Get Pregnant: Boost Your Chances of Conceiving

Medically reviewed by Dr. Shuchita Batra, MBBS, MS (Obstetrics & Gynaecology)

Find your fertile days - MiGynae App

A free period and ovulation tracker, plus doctor-backed guidance for trying to conceive.

A few simple, proven steps make a real difference when you are trying for a baby. Here is where to start - and how the app helps you every cycle.

For most healthy couples, conceiving takes time - often several months - and that is normal. A few simple things can genuinely improve your chances. Start here.

Know your fertile window

This is the single biggest lever. You are most fertile in the few days around ovulation - usually the middle of your cycle. Timing intercourse to these days matters more than anything else.

Find your fertile days - track them

The MiGynae App has a period and ovulation tracker. Log your cycle and it predicts your fertile window and ovulation day for you - so you know exactly when to try. Start using it from this cycle.

Time it right

Have intercourse every 2 to 3 days through the month - this covers your fertile window naturally.

Around ovulation, aim for the day of and the 1 to 2 days before.

Do not stress over "perfect" timing - regular, relaxed trying works best.

Get your body ready

Start folic acid before you conceive (as your doctor advises) - it protects the baby's spine from the earliest days.

Aim for a healthy weight - both too high and too low can affect fertility.

Eat balanced, home-cooked meals with protein, iron and fruit.

Sleep well, move daily, and lower stress where you can.

Cut out smoking and alcohol, and limit caffeine.

When to see a doctor

Get checked sooner, do not just keep waiting, if:

  • You are under 35 and have tried for 12 months
  • You are 35 or older and have tried for 6 months
  • Your periods are irregular or absent, or you have PCOS, thyroid or other conditions
  • You have had miscarriages or any pelvic surgery or infection

Keep tracking, every cycle

Trying takes patience. Keep logging in the MiGynae App period and ovulation tracker - a clear record of your cycles helps you time each month, and gives your doctor the full picture if you ever need one. Use it every cycle until you conceive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fertile window and how is it calculated?
The fertile window comprises the 5 days leading up to ovulation plus ovulation day itself. Sperm can survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, whereas the released egg survives for 12–24 hours.
When is the best time to have intercourse to get pregnant?
Having intercourse every 2 to 3 days throughout the month ensures continuous sperm viability, while having sex on the day of ovulation and the 2 days prior offers the highest mathematical probability of conception.
Why should women take folic acid before getting pregnant?
Taking 400 mcg of folic acid daily starting at least 1 month before conception prevents major neural tube birth defects of the baby's brain and spine (like spina bifida and anencephaly) by up to 70%.
How long does it typically take a healthy couple to conceive?
About 80% to 85% of healthy couples conceive within 1 year of regular unprotected intercourse, and about 90% within 2 years. Taking several months is completely standard.
When should we consult a fertility doctor or gynaecologist?
Seek evaluation if you have been trying for 12 months (if under age 35) or for 6 months (if age 35 or older), or immediately if cycles are absent/irregular, or you have a history of PCOS, endometriosis, or pelvic infections.
