Pregnancy Scan Schedule in India: Dating, NT, TIFFA & Growth Scans

Medically reviewed by Dr. Shuchita Batra, MBBS, MD (Obstetrics & Gynaecology)

The Importance of Prenatal Ultrasound Scans

Ultrasound scans use harmless high-frequency sound waves to create visual images of your developing baby. In India, prenatal diagnostic scans follow structured clinical guidelines established by FOGSI and international societies to monitor viability, anatomy, and growth.

1. Dating & Viability Scan (Weeks 6–9)

Performed early in the first trimester to confirm intrauterine pregnancy, evaluate cardiac activity (fetal heartbeat), rule out ectopic pregnancy, and accurately calculate your Expected Due Date (EDD) based on crown-rump length (CRL).

2. NT / NB Scan & Dual Marker (Weeks 11–13+6)

A crucial first-trimester screening evaluating the fluid translucency behind baby's neck (Nuchal Translucency) and the nasal bone (NB). Combined with maternal blood tests (PAPP-A and Free beta-hCG), it calculates statistical probabilities for chromosomal conditions such as Down syndrome (Trisomy 21).

3. Anomaly Scan / TIFFA (Weeks 18–20)

The Targeted Imaging for Fetal Anomalies (TIFFA) or Level-II anomaly scan is one of the most vital evaluations in pregnancy. The sonologist systematically examines every organ—brain ventricles, four heart chambers, spine integrity, kidneys, stomach bubble, facial profile, and limbs.

4. Growth & Doppler Scans (Weeks 28–36)

Third-trimester ultrasound evaluations assess fetal growth percentiles, estimated fetal weight (EFW), amniotic fluid levels (AFI), and placental maturity. Umbilical artery and uterine artery Doppler studies confirm that blood flows smoothly to your baby.
