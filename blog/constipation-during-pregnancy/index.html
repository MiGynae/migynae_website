Constipation During Pregnancy: Natural Remedies & Safe Relief

Medically reviewed by Dr. Shuchita Batra, MBBS, MD (Obstetrics & Gynaecology)

Understanding Pregnancy Constipation

Constipation affects nearly half of all women during pregnancy. High circulating levels of progesterone relax the smooth muscles throughout your body, including the digestive tract. This slows transit time, allowing the colon to absorb more water and leaving stools harder and more difficult to pass.

Key Contributing Factors

  • Progesterone Hormones: Slows peristaltic bowel contractions.
  • Prenatal Iron Supplements: Essential for preventing maternal anemia, but iron formulations can irritate and slow bowel movement.
  • Expanding Uterus: In the second and third trimesters, the anatomical weight of the uterus exerts physical pressure on the rectum.

Proven Indian Dietary Remedies

  • Hydration Priority: Drink 2.5 to 3 liters of water daily. Warm water upon waking stimulates the gastrocolic reflex.
  • High-Fiber Grains: Incorporate daliya (cracked wheat), oats, red rice, and jowar/bajra rotis into daily meals.
  • Natural Laxative Fruits: Ripe papaya (moderately in 2nd/3rd trimester under medical guidance), stewed prunes, pears, apples with peel, and soaked raisins.
  • Isabgol (Psyllium Husk): 1 teaspoon of Isabgol in a glass of warm water or milk at bedtime provides gentle, non-habit-forming bulk fiber (always check with your doctor first).

Never take over-the-counter chemical stimulant laxatives without consulting your OBGYN, as some can stimulate uterine irritability.
