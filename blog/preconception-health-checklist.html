Preconception Health Checklist: Essential Steps Before Getting Pregnant

Medically reviewed by Dr. Shuchita Batra, MBBS, MD (Obstetrics & Gynaecology)

Planning for Pregnancy: Where to Begin

The journey to parenthood begins long before you see two pink lines on a home test kit. Optimizing your maternal health before conception significantly reduces risks of complications such as neural tube defects, gestational hypertension, and maternal anaemia.

1. Start Folic Acid Supplementation

Gynecologists strongly recommend taking 400 to 500 mcg of folic acid daily starting at least 1 to 3 months before attempting conception. Folic acid plays an indispensable role in neural tube development during the very first 28 days of gestation—often before a woman even realizes she is pregnant.

2. Schedule a Pre-Pregnancy Health Checkup

Consult with your OBGYN for pre-pregnancy blood screenings:

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) & Haemoglobin: Anemia is remarkably common among Indian women and should be corrected early.
  • Rubella & Hepatitis B Immunity: Testing antibodies ensures protection against infections that can adversely impact fetal organ development.
  • Blood Sugar (HbA1c / Fasting Glucose): Early detection of prediabetes or insulin resistance allows for dietary calibration prior to pregnancy.
  • Thyroid Profile (TSH): Maternal thyroid health is crucial for early embryonic neurodevelopment.

3. Review Current Medications

Certain routine medications for acne, blood pressure, or migraines are contraindicated during pregnancy. Never stop prescribed therapies abruptly; instead, discuss pregnancy-safe alternatives with your doctor.

4. Dental Evaluation

Hormonal surges in pregnancy exacerbate gum inflammation and gingivitis. Periodontal infection has been clinically linked with preterm labour. Undergoing a thorough dental cleaning and addressing cavities beforehand ensures safety.

5. Healthy Weight & Diet Adjustments

Achieving a balanced body mass index (BMI) promotes regular ovulation. Emphasize home-cooked Indian meals rich in lentils, seasonal green vegetables, paneer, eggs, seeds, and nuts while limiting excess refined sugar and trans-fats.
