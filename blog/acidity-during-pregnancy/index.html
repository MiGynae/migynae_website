Acidity and Heartburn in Pregnancy: Causes and Indian Home Remedies

Medically reviewed by Dr. Shuchita Batra, MBBS, MD (Obstetrics & Gynaecology)

Why Does Acidity Spike During Pregnancy?

Heartburn and severe acid reflux are frequent complaints among Indian expectant mothers, especially from the late second trimester onwards. The culprit is twofold: progesterone relaxes the lower esophageal sphincter (the muscular valve preventing stomach acid from creeping up into the esophagus), and your growing baby physically pushes stomach contents upwards.

Effective Lifestyle Hacks to Prevent Reflux

  • Eat Smaller, Frequent Meals: Divide your daily nutrition into 5 to 6 small portions rather than 3 heavy meals. Overfilling the stomach exacerbates backflow.
  • Remain Upright After Eating: Avoid lying down for at least 60 to 90 minutes post-meal. A gentle 15-minute stroll indoors aids digestion.
  • Elevate Your Head at Night: Prop your head and upper torso up with an extra pillow or pregnancy wedge to use gravity against reflux.

Traditional, Safe Soothing Remedies

  • Cold Milk / Buttermilk: A few sips of chilled skim milk or fresh chaas (buttermilk) prepared with roasted cumin (jeera) neutralizes acute acid spikes.
  • Fennel Seeds (Saunf): Chewing half a teaspoon of plain roasted saunf after meals stimulates salivary enzymes and calms gastric irritation.
  • Coconut Water: A natural electrolyte drink that cools the digestive tract and hydrates without adding heavy acidity.

If severe reflux interferes with sleep or your ability to eat, consult your doctor. Safe antacid syrups and pregnancy-approved H2 blockers are readily available.
