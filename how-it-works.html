How the MiGynae Pregnancy App Works

From download to daily tracking — here's exactly how expecting mothers use MiGynae throughout pregnancy.

1

Download & Create Your Profile

Install MiGynae free from Google Play or the App Store. Enter your due date (or last menstrual period) so the app personalizes weekly updates, diet plans, and trimester content from day one.

2

Use Daily Tracking & Learn

Open the app each day for your week-by-week update. Log kicks when advised, start the contraction timer if labour begins, browse nutrition charts, and search the medical Q&A library anytime a question comes up.

3

Prepare for Birth & Recovery

In your third trimester, access labour prep exercises and our free Guides for birth and postpartum. Share tracking history with your doctor at every visit.

What You'll Use Most

Home screen

Weekly baby size, popular symptom topics, and quick search for pregnancy questions.

Trackers tab

Kick counter and contraction timer in one place — designed for use at 3 AM when you need clarity.

Diet section

Trimester meal plans, Indian superfoods, sample charts, and foods to avoid.

Community

Connect with other expecting mothers (where enabled) for shared experiences and support.
