What's normal — and what's not

Normal

Bleeding like a heavy period, and cramps as the uterus shrinks. Discharge (lochia) fades over weeks: red → pink → brown → yellow.

Not normal — get care

Soaking a pad every hour, foul-smelling discharge, or large clots. Tell your gynaecologist.

Get medical help now if you notice

Any one of these — don't wait it out.

  • Heavy bleeding — a pad soaked within an hour
  • Fever
  • Foul smell or abnormal discharge
  • Redness, pus, or a gap in your stitches
  • Increasing pain or swelling
  • Severe difficulty sitting or walking
2

Quick do's & don'ts

Do
  • Keep sipping fluids
  • Start gentle walking (as advised)
  • Kegels daily
  • Bend from the knees to lift baby
  • Clean front to back
Avoid
  • Tight waistbands over stitches
  • Straining / constipation
  • A wet or sweaty incision
  • Ignoring worsening pain
  • Complete bed rest
